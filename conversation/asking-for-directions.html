Asking for Directions

Characters:

  • BEN: A friendly local walking his dog.
  • MEI: A newcomer trying to find the public library.

Scene: Mei is new in town and trying to find the public library. Her phone died, so she asks Ben, a passerby, for directions.

Conversation

MEI: Excuse me! I’m sorry to bother you, but I’m lost. Could you help me?

BEN: Of course! Where are you trying to go?

MEI: The public library. My phone died, so I can’t use maps.

BEN: No problem — it’s not far from here. You’re actually going the right way!

MEI: Oh good! How do I get there from here?

BEN: Keep walking straight for two blocks until you reach the traffic light.

MEI: Straight for two blocks to the traffic light. Then what?

BEN: Turn left on Oak Street. You’ll pass a coffee shop on your right.

MEI: Left on Oak Street, past the coffee shop. Got it.

BEN: Then walk one more block. The library is the big brick building on the corner — you can’t miss it.

MEI: How long will it take to walk there?

BEN: About ten minutes at a normal pace. Are you going to get a library card?

MEI: Yes! I just moved here. Is the library free?

BEN: Completely free. Bring a photo ID and something with your address, like a utility bill.

MEI: Perfect. Is there a landmark I should look for, in case I get confused?

BEN: Good thinking. Right across from the library there’s a park with a big fountain. If you see the fountain, you’re there.

MEI: A fountain across the street — I’ll remember that. Thank you so much, Ben!

BEN: Anytime! Oh — I’m Ben, by the way. Welcome to the neighborhood!

MEI: I’m Mei. Thanks again. By the way, what’s your dog’s name? He’s adorable.

BEN: This is Biscuit. He says welcome too!

MEI: Hi, Biscuit! Okay, straight two blocks, left on Oak, library on the corner. I’ve got it!

BEN: You’ve got it. Enjoy the library, Mei!

— THE END —

Vocabulary List

  • Lost /lɔːst/ (adj.) not knowing where you are. Example: I’m lost. Could you help me?
  • Passerby /ˌpæsərˈbaɪ/ (n.) a person walking past. Example: She asks a passerby.
  • Keep walking straight /kiːp ˈwɔːkɪŋ streɪt/ (phr.) continue in the same direction. Example: Keep walking straight for two blocks.
  • Block /blɑːk/ (n.) the distance between two streets. Example: Walk two blocks.
  • Turn left /tɜːrn left/ (phr.) to go in the left direction. Example: Turn left on Oak Street.
  • Landmark /ˈlændmɑːrk/ (n.) an easily recognized place. Example: Is there a landmark I should look for?
  • You can’t miss it /juː kænt mɪs ɪt/ (phr.) it’s very easy to find. Example: The big brick building — you can’t miss it.
  • At a normal pace /æt ə ˈnɔːrməl peɪs/ (phr.) walking at regular speed. Example: Ten minutes at a normal pace.
  • Fountain /ˈfaʊntən/ (n.) a decorative stream of water. Example: A park with a big fountain.
  • Adorable /əˈdɔːrəbəl/ (adj.) very cute. Example: Your dog is adorable.
