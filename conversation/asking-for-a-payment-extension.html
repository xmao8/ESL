Asking for a Payment Extension

Characters:

  • CHRIS: A billing representative, understanding and professional.
  • AMARA: A hotel housekeeper asking for more time to pay her bill.

Scene: Amara’s hours were cut at work last month, and her electricity bill is due tomorrow. She’s calling to ask for a payment extension. Chris answers.

Conversation

CHRIS: Hello, you’ve reached PowerPlus billing. This is Chris. How can I help?

AMARA: Hi, Chris. My name is Amara Diallo. My bill is due tomorrow, but I can’t pay the full amount right now.

CHRIS: I’m sorry to hear that, Ms. Diallo. Let me pull up your account. Can I have your account number?

AMARA: Yes, it’s 7-7-2-0-4-5-9-1.

CHRIS: Thank you. I see your balance is $184.30, due tomorrow.

AMARA: That’s right. My hours were cut at work last month, so money is very tight. Could I get a payment extension?

CHRIS: I understand — that happens to a lot of people. We can offer a 14-day extension, which moves your due date to the 30th.

AMARA: That would help a lot. I get paid on the 28th, so I can pay the full amount then.

CHRIS: Perfect. I’ll note that on your account. There’s no fee for a one-time extension.

AMARA: No fee? That’s a relief. Will this affect my credit?

CHRIS: No. As long as you pay by the new date, it won’t be reported as late.

AMARA: What if something happens and I still can’t pay by the 30th?

CHRIS: Then call us before the 30th, and we can discuss a payment plan — splitting the bill into smaller amounts over a few months.

AMARA: That’s good to know. I hope it won’t come to that, but it’s comforting to have options.

CHRIS: Absolutely. We’d always rather work with you than send the account to collections.

AMARA: I appreciate that. Can you send me confirmation of the new due date?

CHRIS: I’ll send a text and an email right now. You should receive them in a few minutes.

AMARA: Thank you, Chris. I was really nervous about making this call.

CHRIS: You did the right thing by calling, Ms. Diallo. We’re here to help. Take care!

— THE END —

Vocabulary List

  • Payment extension /ˈpeɪmənt ɪkˈstenʃən/ (n.) extra time to pay a bill. Example: Could I get a payment extension?
  • Due /duː/ (adj.) needing to be paid now. Example: My bill is due tomorrow.
  • Balance /ˈbæləns/ (n.) the amount owed. Example: Your balance is $184.30.
  • Hours were cut /ˈaʊərz wɜːr kʌt/ (phr.) given less work time. Example: My hours were cut at work.
  • Tight /taɪt/ (adj.) not having much money (informal). Example: Money is very tight right now.
  • One-time /wʌn taɪm/ (adj.) happening only once. Example: No fee for a one-time extension.
  • Payment plan /ˈpeɪmənt plæn/ (n.) paying a bill in smaller parts over time. Example: We can discuss a payment plan.
  • Collections /kəˈlekʃənz/ (n.) a company that collects unpaid debts. Example: Rather than send it to collections.
  • Come to that /kʌm tə ðæt/ (phr.) to reach that bad situation. Example: I hope it won’t come to that.
  • Note /noʊt/ (v.) to write down officially. Example: I’ll note that on your account.
