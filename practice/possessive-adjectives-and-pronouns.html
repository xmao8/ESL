Possessive Adjectives and Pronouns Practice

Instructions: Fill in the blank with the correct possessive adjective or possessive pronoun from the choices provided.

Name: _________________________
Date: _________________________
1. She keeps (her / hers) work space very clean.
2. She never eats at (her / hers) desk, but they always eat at (their / theirs).
3. That office is (you / yours).
4. (Theirs / Their) company has more employees than his.
5. That's (your / yours) book. Where is (my / mine)?
6. We will give you (our / ours) plan so you can compare it with (your / yours).
7. The dog loves (its / it's) new toy.
8. Is this car (your / yours) or (our / ours)?
9. (His / Him) report was more detailed than (my / mine).
10. They brought (their / theirs) own lunches, but we forgot (our / ours).
11. I think this jacket is (her / hers).
12. (Whose / Who's) pen is this? Is it (your / yours)?
13. The cat chased (its / it's) tail.
14. (Our / Ours) team played better than (their / theirs).
15. He shared (his / him) ideas, and I shared (my / mine).
16. The house lost (its / it's) roof in the storm.
17. Don't touch that; it's (my / mine)!
18. They finished (their / theirs) work early.
19. This laptop is (my / mine), but that one is (her / hers).
20. Do you know if this phone is (her / hers)?
