Modals for Advice — Time Management

How it works

Use modals to give advice. Should gives strong advice about a good idea. Shouldn't gives strong advice against a bad idea. Could suggests a possibility.

Examples: You should make a to-do list.  ·  You shouldn't stay up late.  ·  You could try a calendar app.
