Last Saturday, my friend Anna invited me to her house for dinner. She said, “Would you like to come at seven o’clock?” I was very excited because Anna is a great cook. When I arrived, she welcomed me warmly and said, “I’m so glad you came.” We had pasta, salad, and bread for dinner; everything was delicious. After dinner, we watched a movie called The Secret Garden. It was really good. I asked Anna, “Have you seen it before?” She laughed and answered, “Yes, but I still love it.” We finished the evening with coffee and cake. What a wonderful night!
