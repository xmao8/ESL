Wiring Money to Family Abroad

Characters:

  • JENNY: A clerk at a money transfer office, efficient and friendly.
  • IBRAHIM: A security guard sending money to his mother abroad.

Scene: Ibrahim wants to send money to his mother overseas. He’s at a money transfer office talking to Jenny, the clerk.

Conversation

JENNY: Hi! Welcome in. Are you sending money or picking some up today?

IBRAHIM: Sending, please. I want to send $300 to my mother. She lives in another country.

JENNY: I can help with that. Which country is she in?

IBRAHIM: Somalia. Can she pick it up in cash there?

JENNY: Yes — we have pickup locations all over Somalia. She can collect cash with a photo ID.

IBRAHIM: How long does it take for the money to arrive?

JENNY: Usually within minutes. In some areas it can take up to a few hours.

IBRAHIM: That’s fast. What’s the fee for sending $300?

JENNY: The fee is $8.99, so your total today is $308.99.

IBRAHIM: Okay. Will my mother receive exactly $300?

JENNY: She’ll receive the equivalent in her local currency. The exchange rate today is posted right here on the screen.

IBRAHIM: I see. Do I need to bring any ID?

JENNY: Yes, I’ll need your photo ID — a driver’s license or passport works.

IBRAHIM: Here’s my driver’s license. What information do you need about my mother?

JENNY: Her full name exactly as it appears on her ID, and her phone number so we can text her the pickup code.

IBRAHIM: Her name is Faduma Hassan. Let me spell that: F-A-D-U-M-A, H-A-S-S-A-N.

JENNY: Got it. And is this your first time sending money with us?

IBRAHIM: Yes. Is there anything else I should know?

JENNY: Just keep your receipt with the tracking number. If your mother has any trouble, she can call our local office with that number.

IBRAHIM: Perfect. Here’s my card for payment.

JENNY: All set! Your mother should get a text within the hour. Anything else I can do for you?

IBRAHIM: No, thank you, Jenny. You made this very simple.

JENNY: You’re welcome! Come back anytime.

— THE END —

Vocabulary List

  • Wire /waɪər/ (v.) to send money electronically. Example: I want to wire $300 to my mother.
  • Abroad /əˈbrɔːd/ (adv.) in another country. Example: Sending money to family abroad.
  • Pickup location /ˈpɪkʌp loʊˈkeɪʃən/ (n.) a place to collect money. Example: We have pickup locations all over.
  • Equivalent /ɪˈkwɪvələnt/ (n.) an equal amount in another currency. Example: She’ll receive the equivalent in local currency.
  • Exchange rate /ɪksˈtʃeɪndʒ reɪt/ (n.) the value of one currency in another. Example: The exchange rate is on the screen.
  • Photo ID /ˈfoʊtoʊ aɪ diː/ (n.) an ID card with your picture. Example: She can collect it with a photo ID.
  • Exactly as it appears /ɪɡˈzæktli æz ɪt əˈpɪrz/ (phr.) matching precisely. Example: Her name exactly as on her ID.
  • Pickup code /ˈpɪkʌp koʊd/ (n.) a number needed to collect money. Example: We’ll text her the pickup code.
  • Tracking number /ˈtrækɪŋ ˈnʌmbər/ (n.) a code to follow your transfer. Example: Keep your receipt with the tracking number.
  • All set /ɔːl set/ (phr.) finished and ready. Example: All set! She’ll get a text soon.
