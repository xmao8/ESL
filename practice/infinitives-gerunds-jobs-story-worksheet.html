Infinitives & Gerunds — Maya’s New Job

How it works

Maya is starting a new job — but 15 infinitives and gerunds are missing from her story! Read the story carefully and fill in each blank with the correct form. Remember: some verbs take an infinitive (want to become, decide to apply, hope to get), some take a gerund (enjoy helping, love working), and after a preposition always use a gerund (good at talking, interested in learning, tired of waiting). A gerund can also be the subject of a sentence (Working on a team makes me proud).

Examples: She wants to become a dental hygienist.  ·  She enjoys helping people.  ·  She is good at talking to patients.

Read the Story — Fill in 15 Infinitives & Gerunds

✏️ Now use it: Tell your partner about a job you would like to have in 3 sentences — use an infinitive in one, a gerund after a verb in one, and a gerund after a preposition in one. Can your partner guess your dream job?
