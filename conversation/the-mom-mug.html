The "Best Plant Mom" Mug

Scene: A cozy living room with a sofa, coffee table, and a large potted plant on a stand near a window.

(The scene opens. Anna inspects her plant; Ben scrolls on the sofa.)

ANNA: (to the plant) Oh, you are looking just beautiful today. So green, so vibrant. My little piece of nature indoors.

BEN: (without looking up) Still talking to the plant, Anna? You spend more time with that than with me.

ANNA: (smiling, a bit sharp) This plant listens, Ben. And it doesn’t leave its socks on the floor.

BEN: (puts phone down) Fair point. So, what’s the plan for dinner? I’m starving.

ANNA: I was thinking of making that new pasta recipe. I need to go to the market later.

(Knock on the door. Chloe enters with a crushed gift bag.)

CHLOE: Hi everyone! Sorry I’m a bit late. I brought something!

ANNA: Chloe! Come in. What’s that?

CHLOE: It’s a housewarming gift, sort of! I saw it and thought of your living room.

(Chloe walks, trips on the rug, and knocks the plant over. Pot shatters.)

CHLOE: (gasps) Oh my goodness! Anna, I am SO, so sorry! I’m such a klutz!

ANNA: (stares) Oh no… my fern! My beautiful fern!

BEN: Are you okay, Chloe? Did you hurt yourself?

CHLOE: I’m fine. But look at Anna’s plant! I’ve ruined it!

ANNA: (sighs) It’s okay, Chloe. Accidents happen.

BEN: Let’s get a broom and trash bag.

CHLOE: I’ll help! And I will buy you a new plant.

ANNA: (small smile) Thank you. First, let’s clean up.

(Ben exits to get cleaning supplies.)

CHLOE: I promise to be more careful next time.

ANNA: It’s alright. No one got hurt. Maybe a new beginning for the plant.

CHLOE: About this gift…

CHLOE: (holds up bag) It’s a mug! It says “World’s Best Plant Mom.”

ANNA & CHLOE: (laughing)

(Ben returns with broom.)

BEN: What’s so funny?

ANNA: Just a new beginning. Let’s clean up, shall we?
