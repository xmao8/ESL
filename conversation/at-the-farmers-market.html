At the Farmers Market

Characters:

  • TOM: A local farmer selling vegetables at the market, warm and chatty.
  • WEI: A retired teacher exploring the farmers market for the first time.

Scene: It’s Saturday morning. Wei is shopping at the local farmers market for the first time. Tom, a friendly vendor, is selling fresh vegetables.

Conversation

TOM: Morning! Everything on this table was picked yesterday. Can I help you find something?

WEI: Good morning! I’m new to farmers markets. How does it work — do I just pick what I want?

TOM: Exactly! Grab a bag, pick what you like, and I’ll weigh it at the end. Or I can pick the best ones for you.

WEI: These tomatoes look beautiful. Are they organic?

TOM: They sure are. No pesticides — just sunshine, water, and good soil. Taste one!

WEI: Mmm, that’s the best tomato I’ve had in years. The ones at the supermarket don’t taste like anything.

TOM: That’s because ours ripen on the vine. Supermarket tomatoes are picked green and shipped across the country.

WEI: That makes sense. How much are they?

TOM: $3.50 a pound. How many would you like?

WEI: Give me about two pounds, please. And what are these purple vegetables?

TOM: Those are eggplants. They’re great grilled or in stir-fry. Want the recipe I give all my customers?

WEI: Yes, please! I love trying new recipes.

TOM: Slice it, brush with olive oil, add garlic and salt, then grill five minutes per side. Simple and delicious.

WEI: I’ll take two eggplants too. Do you come here every Saturday?

TOM: Every Saturday, rain or shine, from 8 to 1. We also do a vegetable box subscription — $25 a week for a mix of whatever’s fresh.

WEI: A subscription sounds wonderful, but let me start small today. Do you take cards?

TOM: We do — cards, cash, and even phone payments.

WEI: Perfect. Here’s my card. Oh — one more thing. Are these peaches ripe yet?

TOM: Give them one more day on your counter. If they smell sweet at the stem, they’re ready.

WEI: Thank you, Tom! I’ve learned so much. I’ll definitely be back next Saturday.

TOM: See you then, Wei! Tell your friends about us!

— THE END —

Vocabulary List

  • Farmers market /ˈfɑːrmərz ˈmɑːrkɪt/ (n.) an outdoor market where farmers sell directly. Example: She is shopping at the farmers market.
  • Organic /ɔːrˈɡænɪk/ (adj.) grown without chemical pesticides. Example: Are these tomatoes organic?
  • Pesticide /ˈpestəsaɪd/ (n.) a chemical that kills insects on crops. Example: No pesticides — just sunshine and water.
  • Ripen /ˈraɪpən/ (v.) to become ready to eat. Example: Ours ripen on the vine.
  • Weigh /weɪ/ (v.) to measure how heavy something is. Example: I’ll weigh it at the end.
  • A pound /ə paʊnd/ (n.) a unit of weight, about 454 grams. Example: $3.50 a pound.
  • Stir-fry /ˈstɜːr fraɪ/ (n.) a dish of quickly fried vegetables and meat. Example: Great grilled or in stir-fry.
  • Rain or shine /reɪn ɔːr ʃaɪn/ (phr.) no matter what the weather is. Example: Every Saturday, rain or shine.
  • Subscription /səbˈskrɪpʃən/ (n.) a regular paid delivery. Example: We do a vegetable box subscription.
  • Ripe /raɪp/ (adj.) ready to eat. Example: Are these peaches ripe yet?
