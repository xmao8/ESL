Wish and Hope — Thanksgiving & Gratitude

How it works

Wish is for things that are not true now: use the past simple after wish, even though you mean now: I wish I lived closer (I do not live close now). Use could for wishes about ability: I wish I could thank my teacher. With be, use were for all subjects (I wish I were at home). Hope is for things that are possible in the future: use the present simple after hope (I hope my family comes — they might come). Some sentences ask you to choose hope or wish. Read each sentence and type the correct form. The verb to use is in (parentheses).

Examples: I wish I lived closer to my family.  ·  I hope my family comes for Thanksgiving.  ·  I wish my family came. (hope / wish)

Fill in the Blanks (20)

✏️ Now use it: Write 3 sentences about your own wishes and hopes — start one with I wish … (past simple) and one with I hope … (present simple). Read them to a partner.
