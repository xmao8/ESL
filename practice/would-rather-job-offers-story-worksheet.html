Would Rather — Two Job Offers

How it works

Ana has two job offers — one in an office, one outdoors — and she must choose. Read the story and fill in each blank with the verb form that fits. Use would rather + base verb for what Ana prefers (I would rather work outdoors), would rather not for a polite no, and would rather + subject + past when Ana talks about what she wants someone else to do (I would rather you told me about the team). Use Would you rather … or …? to ask a choice.

Examples: I would rather work outdoors than in an office.  ·  I would rather not work on weekends.  ·  Would you rather work in an office or outdoors?  ·  I would rather you told me about the team.

✏️ Now use it: Tell your partner about two jobs you know. Which one would you rather take — and why? Use would rather in every reason.
