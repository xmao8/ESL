Home Repairs & Housing Careers — Vocabulary

How it works

When something breaks in your home, you need the right words to describe the problem and to ask for help. These are also the words people use in housing jobs — the people who fix and manage apartment buildings.

Examples: Our air conditioner isn't working.  ·  The plumber fixed the sink.

Type the correct word or phrase in each blank. There is no word bank — think about which word fits best! Spelling counts, but don't worry: you can try again.

Fill in the Blanks (15)

✏️ Now use it: Which of these jobs would YOU like to do: plumber, electrician, exterminator, or property manager? Write 2 sentences explaining why. Read them to a partner.
