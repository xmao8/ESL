Job Ads & Applications

Setting: Tuesday morning at a job fair. Ana is looking at a job ad on the board, and Luis is filling out an application at the next table. They talk about the ad, the pay, and how to apply. (About 5 minutes of pair speaking practice.)

Words to know:

  • Receptionist /rɪˈsɛpʃənɪst/ (n.) a person who answers the phone and greets people at the front desk. Example: The receptionist at the dental office is very friendly.
  • Bilingual /baɪˈlɪŋɡwəl/ (adj.) able to speak two languages well. Example: The job ad says bilingual workers are wanted.
  • Dependable /dɪˈpɛndəbəl/ (adj.) always on time and doing what you promise. Example: A dependable worker never misses a day of work.
  • Benefits /ˈbɛnəfɪts/ (n.) good things a job gives you, like health insurance or paid vacation. Example: This job offers good benefits and a free lunch.
  • Applicant /ˈæplɪkənt/ (n.) a person who applies for a job. Example: Every applicant must send a resume by Friday.
  • Resume /ˈrɛzəmeɪ/ (n.) a paper that lists your work history and education. Example: Send your resume to the manager by email.
  • References /ˈrɛfərənsɪz/ (n.) people who can tell a boss you are a good worker. Example: Please list two references on your application.
  • Availability /əˌveɪləˈbɪləti/ (n.) the days and hours you are free to work. Example: What is your availability on weekends?
  • Experience /ɪkˈspɪəriəns/ (n.) the work you have done before. Example: Two years of experience is required for this job.
  • Detail-oriented /ˈdiːteɪl ˈɔːriɛntɪd/ (adj.) careful about every small thing. Example: A detail-oriented worker checks the numbers twice.

Dialogue: At the Job Fair

Ana: Luis, look at this ad! A receptionist is needed at a dental office.

Luis: Really? What is the pay?

Ana: It says $14–16 an hour, full time. And good benefits are offered.

Luis: What hours? Is experience required?

Ana: The office is open from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Experience is required, but training is provided.

Luis: That sounds good. How do I apply?

Ana: Applications must be sent by Friday. You can send your resume by email.

Luis: What should I write on the application?

Ana: Your personal information, your employment history, and your availability. And two references are needed.

Luis: I have a question. Is the job bilingual? My Spanish is very good.

Ana: Yes! The ad says bilingual workers are wanted. You should apply today.

Luis: You are right. I will send my resume tonight.

🗣️ Speaking challenge — role play (about 5 minutes)

  • Student A is the manager. Read a job ad to your partner. Tell them what is required: the hours, the pay, and the benefits.
  • Student B is the applicant. Ask three questions about the job. Use the passive voice: “Is experience required?” “When must applications be sent?”
  • Then swap roles and try a different job: auto technician, pharmacy clerk, custodian, or administrative assistant.
  • Use at least three of the words above in your talk.
