Comparatives & Superlatives — Smart Shopping

How it works

Use comparatives to compare two things (cheaper, more expensive) and superlatives to compare three or more (the cheapest, the most expensive).

Examples: This phone is cheaper than that one.  ·  This is the cheapest phone in the store.
