A Fresh Start

Characters:

  • NAOMI: 29, an office worker who feels tired and wants to change her lifestyle.
  • ALEX: Naomi’s friend, an energetic yoga instructor.
  • MRS. LOPEZ: Naomi’s elderly neighbor who loves gardening and home cooking.

Scene 1: Apartment hallway, Saturday morning. Mrs. Lopez waters plants; Naomi walks past with instant noodles.

Scene 1

MRS. LOPEZ: Good morning, Naomi! You look tired again. Long week?

NAOMI: (yawns) Yeah. Work was crazy. I didn’t even have time to cook. Instant noodles again!

MRS. LOPEZ: Oh dear, that’s not healthy food. Come, I have fresh vegetables from my garden.

NAOMI: (smiles weakly) Thanks, Mrs. Lopez, but I don’t know what to do with them. I don’t even have energy to cook properly.

MRS. LOPEZ: You’re too young to feel this tired. Maybe some exercise would help!

Alex jogs up the stairs, cheerful.

ALEX: Morning, ladies! Naomi, I was just going to invite you to my weekend yoga class. It’s free!

NAOMI: Yoga? Me? I can barely touch my toes.

ALEX: That’s why you should come! Healthy living starts with small steps.

Scene 2

Community park, Sunday morning. Birds sing; a small group stretches on yoga mats.

ALEX: Welcome! Today we’ll focus on breathing and gentle movement. Don’t worry, everyone starts somewhere.

NAOMI: (tries to follow) Wow, this is harder than it looks!

ALEX: You’re doing fine. Just breathe deeply. Feel your body relax.

MRS. LOPEZ: (in the back row) This is wonderful! My back already feels better.

NAOMI: (laughing) Mrs. Lopez, you’re more flexible than me!

MRS. LOPEZ: Gardening keeps me young, dear. I move every day, and I eat what I grow.

ALEX: See? Movement and good food are the perfect pair.

Scene 3

Naomi’s apartment, one week later. Soup simmers; Naomi chops vegetables.

NAOMI: I can’t believe I’m cooking real food!

MRS. LOPEZ: It smells delicious. You see what fresh ingredients can do?

ALEX: (entering) And look at you—bright eyes, big smile! How’s the yoga going?

NAOMI: I actually look forward to it now. I even walk to work sometimes.

ALEX: That’s fantastic. The secret is balance—eat well, move your body, rest, and enjoy life.

NAOMI: (serving soup) I feel lighter, happier, and more awake. I guess “healthy living” isn’t about being perfect.

MRS. LOPEZ: Exactly. It’s about loving yourself enough to take care of yourself.

ALEX: Cheers to that—and to a fresh start!

— THE END —

Vocabulary List

  • Lifestyle /ˈlaɪfstaɪl/ (n.) the way a person lives. Example: She wants to change her lifestyle.
  • Energy /ˈenərdʒi/ (n.) strength to do activities. Example: Healthy food gives you energy.
  • Flexible /ˈfleksɪbl/ (adj.) able to bend or move easily. Example: Yoga can make you more flexible.
  • Breathe /briːð/ (v.) to take air into and out of the lungs. Example: Breathe deeply to relax.
  • Ingredient /ɪnˈɡriːdiənt/ (n.) a food used to make a dish. Example: Fresh ingredients improve flavor.
  • Balance /ˈbæl.əns/ (n.) a healthy mix of different things. Example: Find a balance between work and rest.
  • Gardening /ˈɡɑːrdnɪŋ/ (n.) growing plants for food or beauty. Example: Gardening keeps Mrs. Lopez active.
  • Simmer /ˈsɪmər/ (v.) to cook just below boiling. Example: The soup simmers on the stove.
  • Movement /ˈmuːvmənt/ (n.) physical activity of the body. Example: Daily movement improves health.
  • Relax /rɪˈlæks/ (v.) to rest and feel calm. Example: Deep breathing helps you relax.
  • Consistency /kənˈsɪstənsi/ (n.) doing the same good action again and again. Example: Consistency builds strong habits.
