Small Talk in the Break Room

Characters:

  • KEVIN: An IT support specialist, outgoing and chatty.
  • LUCIA: A customer service representative, warm but a bit shy.

Scene: Kevin and Lucia both happen to be in the break room at lunchtime. They work in different departments and don’t know each other well.

Conversation

KEVIN: Hey! You’re from customer service, right? I’m Kevin, from IT.

LUCIA: Hi, Kevin! I’m Lucia. I think we met at the holiday party.

KEVIN: That’s right! How’s your day going so far?

LUCIA: Busy! Mondays are always crazy with weekend messages. How about you?

KEVIN: Same here — three password resets before 10 a.m. Is that your lunch? It smells amazing.

LUCIA: Thanks! It’s my grandmother’s rice recipe. Do you usually bring lunch or buy it?

KEVIN: I bring leftovers most days. There’s a great taco truck on Fridays though — have you tried it?

LUCIA: Not yet! Where does it park?

KEVIN: Right on the corner of 5th and Main. Get there before noon or the line is huge.

LUCIA: I’ll check it out this Friday. So, have you been with the company long?

KEVIN: About two years. You?

LUCIA: Eight months. I moved here from Texas for this job.

KEVIN: Oh nice! How are you liking California so far?

LUCIA: I love the weather, but I miss real barbecue. And my family, of course.

KEVIN: I bet. Do you get back to Texas often?

LUCIA: A few times a year. My mom keeps asking when I’m visiting next. What about you — are you from around here?

KEVIN: Born and raised! But I’ve never been to Texas. I should visit sometime.

LUCIA: You should! I’ll give you a list of barbecue places. Hey, do you know if the company is doing anything for the holidays this year?

KEVIN: I heard there might be a potluck in December. Last year’s was fun — someone brought amazing dumplings.

LUCIA: A potluck sounds perfect. I could bring my grandmother’s rice!

KEVIN: I’d sign up for that. Well, I should get back to my desk. It was great chatting with you, Lucia!

LUCIA: You too, Kevin! See you around — and thanks for the taco truck tip!

— THE END —

Vocabulary List

  • Small talk /smɔːl tɔːk/ (n.) friendly, light conversation. Example: They make small talk in the break room.
  • Break room /breɪk ruːm/ (n.) a room where workers rest and eat. Example: They met in the break room.
  • Leftovers /ˈleftoʊvərz/ (n.) food remaining from an earlier meal. Example: I bring leftovers most days.
  • Taco truck /ˈtɑːkoʊ trʌk/ (n.) a food truck selling tacos. Example: There’s a great taco truck on Fridays.
  • Born and raised /bɔːrn ænd reɪzd/ (phr.) born and grew up in a place. Example: Born and raised in California!
  • Potluck /ˈpɑːtlʌk/ (n.) a meal where everyone brings a dish. Example: There might be a potluck in December.
  • Chatty /ˈtʃæti/ (adj.) talkative and friendly. Example: Kevin is outgoing and chatty.
  • Crazy /ˈkreɪzi/ (adj.) very busy or hectic (informal). Example: Mondays are always crazy.
  • Tip /tɪp/ (n.) a helpful suggestion. Example: Thanks for the taco truck tip!
  • Sign up /saɪn ʌp/ (phr v.) to register for something. Example: I’d sign up for that.
