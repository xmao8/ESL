Making a Doctor’s Appointment by Phone

Characters:

  • MS. LEE: Receptionist at Green Valley Family Clinic, polite and efficient.
  • CARLOS: A warehouse worker who needs an annual checkup.

Scene: Carlos is at home, calling his family clinic to schedule a checkup. Ms. Lee, the receptionist, answers the phone.

Conversation

The phone rings twice, then Ms. Lee picks up.

MS. LEE: Good morning, Green Valley Family Clinic. This is Ms. Lee. How may I help you?

CARLOS: Good morning. I’d like to make an appointment for a checkup, please.

MS. LEE: Of course. Are you a new patient or have you been here before?

CARLOS: I’ve been there before. My name is Carlos Rivera.

MS. LEE: Thank you, Mr. Rivera. Let me pull up your file. Can you confirm your date of birth?

CARLOS: Sure, it’s March 14th, 1988.

MS. LEE: Got it. What kind of appointment do you need — just a general checkup?

CARLOS: Yes, just my annual physical. Nothing urgent.

MS. LEE: All right. Dr. Adams has openings next Tuesday at 9:30 in the morning or Thursday at 2:15 in the afternoon. Which works better for you?

CARLOS: Tuesday morning works better. I start work at noon.

MS. LEE: Perfect. I’ll book you for Tuesday, September 29th at 9:30 a.m. with Dr. Adams.

CARLOS: Great. Do I need to bring anything?

MS. LEE: Please bring your photo ID and your insurance card. And try to arrive about fifteen minutes early to fill out the forms.

CARLOS: Okay. Will I need to fast before the appointment? You know, no food or water?

MS. LEE: For a general physical, fasting isn’t required. But if the doctor orders blood work, we’ll schedule that separately.

CARLOS: Understood. Can I ask — is there parking at the clinic?

MS. LEE: Yes, there’s a free parking lot right behind the building.

CARLOS: Perfect. One more thing — what’s your cancellation policy, in case something comes up at work?

MS. LEE: We just ask that you call us at least 24 hours ahead if you need to cancel or reschedule.

CARLOS: That’s fair. Could you please repeat the date and time once more?

MS. LEE: Certainly. Tuesday, September 29th at 9:30 in the morning with Dr. Adams.

CARLOS: Thank you so much, Ms. Lee. See you then.

MS. LEE: You’re welcome, Mr. Rivera. We’ll see you Tuesday. Have a good day!

— THE END —

Vocabulary List

  • Appointment /əˈpɔɪntmənt/ (n.) a planned time to meet someone, such as a doctor. Example: I’d like to make an appointment for a checkup.
  • Checkup /ˈtʃekʌp/ (n.) a general medical exam to make sure you are healthy. Example: I need an appointment for my annual checkup.
  • Receptionist /rɪˈsepʃənɪst/ (n.) the person who answers phones and greets visitors at an office. Example: The receptionist booked my appointment.
  • Confirm /kənˈfɜːrm/ (v.) to say that something is true or correct. Example: Can you confirm your date of birth?
  • Annual /ˈænjuəl/ (adj.) happening once a year. Example: I get an annual physical every September.
  • Physical /ˈfɪzɪkəl/ (n.) a complete medical examination of your body. Example: Just a general physical, nothing urgent.
  • Insurance card /ɪnˈʃʊrəns kɑːrd/ (n.) a card showing you have health insurance. Example: Please bring your photo ID and insurance card.
  • Fast /fæst/ (v.) to not eat or drink for a period of time. Example: Will I need to fast before the appointment?
  • Reschedule /riːˈskedʒuːl/ (v.) to change an appointment to a different time. Example: Call 24 hours ahead if you need to reschedule.
  • Cancellation policy /ˌkænsəˈleɪʃən ˈpɑːləsi/ (n.) the rules about canceling an appointment. Example: What’s your cancellation policy?
