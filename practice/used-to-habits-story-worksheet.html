Used To — Sara’s New Habits

How it works

Used to talks about past habits — things you did before but do not do now. In this story, Sara changed her habits — but 15 used to forms are missing! Read the story carefully and fill in each blank with the form that fits. Use used to + verb for past habits, didn’t use to + verb for negative habits, and use to + verb after Did in questions.

Examples: She used to eat fast food every day.  ·  He didn’t use to exercise.  ·  Did you use to drink soda?
