Shopping for Clothes: Finding the Right Size

Characters:

  • JAKE: A sales associate at a clothing store, friendly and helpful.
  • LINH: An accountant looking for a blouse for a job interview.

Scene: Linh needs a blouse for a job interview tomorrow. She is in a clothing store, looking through a rack. Jake, a sales associate, offers help.

Conversation

JAKE: Hi! Welcome in. Are you looking for anything in particular today?

LINH: Yes, I need a blouse for a job interview tomorrow. Something professional but not too formal.

JAKE: Great, the blouses are right over here. What size do you usually wear?

LINH: In my country I wear a medium, but sizes here seem different.

JAKE: You’re right — sizing varies a lot between brands. Let me grab a few options so you can try them on.

LINH: That would be wonderful. I like this light blue one. Do you have it in medium?

JAKE: Let me check… Here’s a medium. The fitting rooms are just around the corner.

Linh tries on the blouse and comes out of the fitting room.

LINH: Hmm, the medium is a little tight across the shoulders. Do you have a large?

JAKE: Of course. Here’s the large — and I also brought the same style in white, in case you want to compare.

LINH: Thank you! I’ll try both.

A few minutes later, Linh comes out smiling.

LINH: The large fits much better. It’s comfortable, and I can move my arms easily. I’ll take the blue one.

JAKE: Excellent choice — the color really suits you. Is there anything else you need for the interview?

LINH: Actually, do you have any return policy in case I change my mind?

JAKE: Yes, you have 30 days to return or exchange it, as long as the tags are still on and you have the receipt.

LINH: Good to know. Is this blouse on sale? The tag says $39.99, but there’s a red sticker too.

JAKE: Good eye! That sticker means it’s an extra 20% off at the register.

LINH: Even better. Can I pay with a credit card?

JAKE: Absolutely. Right this way — I’ll ring you up.

At the register, Jake folds the blouse and places it in a bag.

JAKE: Here you go. Good luck at your interview tomorrow!

LINH: Thank you so much for all your help, Jake.

JAKE: Anytime! Come back and see us again.

— THE END —

Vocabulary List

  • Blouse /blaʊs/ (n.) a shirt for women, often worn for work. Example: I need a blouse for a job interview.
  • Rack /ræk/ (n.) a frame where clothes hang in a store. Example: She is looking through a rack.
  • Try on /traɪ ɑːn/ (phr v.) to put on clothes to check the fit before buying. Example: You can try them on in the fitting room.
  • Fitting room /ˈfɪtɪŋ ruːm/ (n.) a small room in a store for trying on clothes. Example: The fitting rooms are around the corner.
  • Tight /taɪt/ (adj.) clothes that fit too closely and feel uncomfortable. Example: The medium is a little tight.
  • Return policy /rɪˈtɜːrn ˈpɑːləsi/ (n.) the store’s rules for bringing items back. Example: Do you have a return policy?
  • Exchange /ɪksˈtʃeɪndʒ/ (v.) to replace a bought item with a different one. Example: You have 30 days to return or exchange it.
  • On sale /ɑːn seɪl/ (phr.) being sold at a lower price. Example: Is this blouse on sale?
  • Register /ˈredʒɪstər/ (n.) the counter where you pay in a store. Example: It’s an extra 20% off at the register.
  • Ring up /rɪŋ ʌp/ (phr v.) to enter a purchase into the cash register. Example: I’ll ring you up over here.
