Have To / Must — Smart Shopping Rules

How it works

Use must and have to for rules and strong obligation, don't have to / doesn't have to when something is not necessary, and mustn't when something is prohibited.

Examples: You must keep your receipt.  ·  You don't have to pay extra.  ·  You mustn't open it first.
