Looking at an Apartment to Rent

Characters:

  • SARAH: A leasing agent, enthusiastic and professional.
  • VICTOR: A bus driver searching for a one-bedroom apartment.

Scene: Victor is looking for a one-bedroom apartment. Sarah, the leasing agent, is showing him a unit.

Conversation

SARAH: Welcome! This is unit 4B — a one-bedroom on the second floor. Come on in!

VICTOR: Thanks. It looks bright. Which way do the windows face?

SARAH: They face east, so you get beautiful morning sun. Let me show you the kitchen first.

VICTOR: The kitchen looks updated. Does it come with the refrigerator and stove?

SARAH: Yes, all appliances are included — fridge, stove, dishwasher, and microwave.

VICTOR: Nice. Is there a washer and dryer in the unit?

SARAH: There are hookups for your own machines, but there’s also a shared laundry room on the first floor. It’s $1.50 per load.

VICTOR: Okay. How about parking? I have a car.

SARAH: One assigned parking spot comes with the unit. Street parking is also free in this neighborhood.

VICTOR: Good. What’s the monthly rent, and what’s included?

SARAH: It’s $1,450 a month. Water and trash are included, but electricity and internet are separate.

VICTOR: Is the building quiet? I work early shifts and sleep in the afternoon.

SARAH: Honest answer: the walls are pretty thick, but you might hear some footsteps from upstairs. Most tenants say it’s quiet.

VICTOR: I appreciate your honesty. Are pets allowed? I have a small dog.

SARAH: Dogs under 25 pounds are allowed with a $300 pet deposit. How big is your dog?

VICTOR: She’s 20 pounds, so we’re good. What’s the application process?

SARAH: Just fill out the application online — there’s a $40 fee for the background and credit check. We usually decide within 48 hours.

VICTOR: And if I’m approved, when could I move in?

SARAH: The unit is available November 1st. We’d need the first month’s rent plus the security deposit to hold it.

VICTOR: This looks promising. I’ll fill out the application tonight.

SARAH: Wonderful! Here’s my card — call me if you have any questions.

— THE END —

Vocabulary List

  • Leasing agent /ˈliːsɪŋ ˈeɪdʒənt/ (n.) a person who rents out apartments. Example: Sarah is the leasing agent.
  • Unit /ˈjuːnət/ (n.) one apartment in a building. Example: This is unit 4B.
  • Appliance /əˈplaɪəns/ (n.) a large home machine like a fridge. Example: All appliances are included.
  • Hookup /ˈhʊkʌp/ (n.) a connection point for a machine. Example: There are hookups for your own machines.
  • Assigned parking /əˈsaɪnd ˈpɑːrkɪŋ/ (n.) a specific parking spot for you. Example: One assigned spot comes with the unit.
  • Pet deposit /pet dɪˈpɑːzɪt/ (n.) extra deposit for having a pet. Example: Dogs need a $300 pet deposit.
  • Application /ˌæpləˈkeɪʃən/ (n.) a form to request something. Example: Fill out the application online.
  • Background check /ˈbækɡraʊnd tʃek/ (n.) a review of your history. Example: A fee for the background and credit check.
  • Approved /əˈpruːvd/ (adj.) officially accepted. Example: If I’m approved, when can I move in?
  • Hold /hoʊld/ (v.) to reserve something. Example: We need a deposit to hold the unit.
