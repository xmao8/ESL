Imperatives & Politeness — Worksheet

Part A – Fill in the blanks (10)

  1. ________ (not / forget) to lock the door.
  2. ________ (please / wait) outside for a minute.
  3. ________ (just / sit) here until I call your name.
  4. ________ (not / run) in the hallway.
  5. ________ (open) the window, it’s hot in here.
  6. ________ (please / write) your name at the top of the paper.
  7. ________ (be) careful when you cross the street.
  8. ________ (not / use) your phone during class.
  9. ________ (just / listen) to the instructions carefully.
  10. ________ (please / help) me carry these boxes.

Part C – Rewriting (10)

  1. Close the window. →
  2. Don’t shout. →
  3. Give me your pen. →
  4. Wait outside. →
  5. Lend me some money. →
  6. Don’t open that box. →
  7. Move your chair. →
  8. Stop talking. →
  9. Tell me the answer. →
  10. Bring me a glass of water. →
