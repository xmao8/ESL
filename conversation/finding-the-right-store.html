Finding the Right Store

Setting: Saturday morning at a coffee shop downtown. Ana's old laptop died last night, and she needs a new one for her online classes. Her friend Ben, who knows a lot about computers, is helping her decide where to buy it. (About 5 minutes of pair speaking practice.)

Characters:

  • Ana — needs a new laptop quickly; worried about spending too much money.
  • Ben — Ana's friend; loves comparing prices and finding good deals.

Ana: My old laptop finally died last night — right in the middle of my homework!

Ben: Oh no! How old was it?

Ana: Almost seven years. Honestly, it lived a long life. But now I need a new one fast.

Ben: I can help you shop. Where are you thinking of buying it?

Ana: I saw an ad for the big-box store — 20% off all laptops this weekend.

Ben: Those sales can be great, but don't rush. Let's compare a few places first.

Ana: Like where? I only know the big stores and the websites.

Ben: There's also that small computer shop on Main Street. The owner really knows his stuff.

Ana: A small shop? Won't their prices be higher?

Ben: Sometimes, but they often include a free warranty and setup help. That can save you money later.

Ana: Hmm, warranty. My last laptop had no warranty, and the repair cost a fortune.

Ben: Exactly. A good return policy matters too. If something breaks in the first month, you want to bring it back easily.

Ana: What about buying online? The prices look cheaper there.

Ben: They can be, but you can't touch the laptop before you buy it. And returns can be a headache.

Ana: True. I want to feel the keyboard before I pay seven hundred dollars.

Ben: Here's a trick: some big-box stores will price match websites. You get the online price with in-store service.

Ana: Oh, that's smart! So I show them the online ad and ask for the same price?

Ben: Yes. Just bring the ad on your phone. It never hurts to ask.

Ana: What about refurbished laptops? I saw some really cheap ones.

Ben: Refurbished can be a bargain, but check the warranty carefully. Some only cover ninety days.

Ana: Got it. So my plan: compare prices online, then visit the big store and the small shop.

Ben: And talk to the salesperson. Ask what's included — warranty, setup, returns.

Ana: You're right. The cheapest price isn't always the best deal.

Ben: Exactly! A smart shopper looks at the whole picture, not just the price tag.

Ana: Okay, let's finish our coffee and go hunting. You're my expert advisor today!

Ben: (Laughs) Just buy me lunch afterward. Deal?

🎯 Speaking Challenge

  • Use 3 new vocabulary words in your conversation.
  • Talk about YOUR life, not just the characters'.
  • After you finish: your teacher will share useful corrections they heard — during the conversation, focus on speaking, not on being perfect.

Vocabulary List

  • Warranty /ˈwɔːrənti/ (n.) a promise from the seller to repair or replace a product if it breaks. Example: The laptop comes with a two-year warranty.
  • Bargain /ˈbɑːrɡɪn/ (n.) something bought for less than its usual price. Example: That refurbished laptop was a real bargain.
  • Compare /kəmˈpɛr/ (v.) to look at two or more things to see how they are the same or different. Example: Let's compare prices before we decide.
  • Salesperson /ˈseɪlzpɜːrsən/ (n.) a person whose job is to sell products in a store. Example: The salesperson answered all our questions.
  • Receipt /rɪˈsiːt/ (n.) a paper or email that proves you paid for something. Example: Keep your receipt in case you need a refund.
  • Return policy /rɪˈtɜːrn ˈpɑːləsi/ (n.) a store's rules about bringing products back. Example: Check the return policy before you buy.
  • Refurbished /ˌriːˈfɜːrbɪʃt/ (adj.) repaired or cleaned so it can be sold again. Example: Refurbished phones cost less than new ones.
  • Price match /praɪs mætʃ/ (v. phrase) to lower a price to equal a competitor's lower price. Example: The store will price match any online ad.
  • Brand /brænd/ (n.) the name of a product made by a particular company. Example: Which brand of laptop do you trust most?
  • Deal /diːl/ (n.) a good price or special offer. Example: I found a great deal on a printer.
